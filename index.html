01 / INTRODUCTION

About me

Hi, I’m Kira Dabbagh, a senior studying Computer Science at Columbia’s School of Engineering and Applied Science (SEAS). I’m from Lebanon, speak English, French, and Arabic, and have been dancing ballet for 18 years.

Most of my experience so far has been on the software side, so working with hardware is entirely new to me. I’m excited to get hands-on, learn how code can control physical devices, and build things that people can interact with beyond a screen.

02 / INTENTIONS

My goals for the class

  1. Bring software into the physical world.Learn to write code for microcontrollers and understand how software works with sensors and other electronic components.
  2. Build confidence with hardware.Get hands-on experience assembling, testing, and troubleshooting physical projects, and become more comfortable learning through trial and error.
  3. Turn ideas into interactive experiences.Explore how thoughtful design and creative coding can make everyday objects respond to the people around them.
